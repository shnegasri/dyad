A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Good questions, clear answers.

Is WellPractice connected to a live AI service?

No. The AI learning centre uses local rules and a small set of sample responses. No external model or AI service is connected.

Are exam dates and syllabus details official?

No. Exam listings and resource counts are fictional demonstration content. Always confirm current dates, eligibility, and syllabus with the official exam authority.

Where is my practice data stored?

Quiz results and demo preferences are stored in localStorage in this browser. They are not sent to a server, and clearing browser data removes them.

Can I use the site offline?

This prototype does not include a service worker or cached resources. Its offline control is only a visual simulation.
